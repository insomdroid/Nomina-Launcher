Nomina is an Android launcher for people who want their home screen to feel calm, intentional, and unmistakably personal. Make space for what matters, keep the things you use close, and shape the details around the way you use your phone.

## Make it yours

### A home screen with room to breathe
- Create multiple home pages and arrange apps exactly where you want them.
- Choose your own icon grid, icon size, labels, and page indicators.
- Add Android widgets and place them where they make sense.
- Scroll between pages with your wallpaper, or keep it still.
- Lock your layout when everything is just right.

### Your apps, within easy reach
- Keep your favourites in a flexible dock with room for up to 10 apps.
- Show or hide the dock, or let it tuck itself away.
- Open a clean app drawer with search, frequently used apps, and optional fast scrolling.
- Hide apps from the drawer without losing the ability to find them through search.
- Organise apps into folders on your home screen and in the app drawer.
- Keep personal and work apps organised with work profile support.

### The Capsule
A compact, customisable shortcut bar that keeps three useful actions close at hand.

- Choose the app for each of the three positions.
- Give each position its own distinctive dot-matrix icon.
- Set up your Capsule around the apps you reach for most.

### Your look, your rules
- Choose from supported icon packs or keep original app icons.
- Personalise icon shapes, including circles, squircles, rounded squares, and more.
- Adjust icon sizes, label sizes, and label colours independently.
- Fine-tune the dock and app drawer backgrounds, colours, and transparency.
- Use your own picture as the app drawer background.
- Add a soft wallpaper blur behind the app drawer when your device supports it.
- Enjoy a clean, expressive interface with light and dark system themes.

### Useful at a glance
- See the date, time, upcoming calendar event, and weather in the home widget.
- Keep battery and network information close without opening another app.
- See notification dots and counts on supported app icons.
- Choose how much notification information appears in the people widget.

### Shortcuts that feel natural
- Swipe up to open your apps.
- Swipe down to reach notifications.
- Double-tap the home screen to lock your device.
- Personalise folder gestures and other everyday interactions.

### Made easier to manage
- Back up your launcher setup and restore it when needed.
- Keep your home screen preferences together in one place.
- Check for updates and share feedback with the project.
