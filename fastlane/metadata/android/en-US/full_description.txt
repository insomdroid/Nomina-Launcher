Nomina is an independent Android home-screen launcher derived from ARK Launcher and maintained as a separate project. It uses Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Material 3. The app interface is currently available in English.

Features:

* Paged home screen with movable app shortcuts and drag-and-drop between pages and the dock.
* App drawer with application search, system-settings search, and calculator / unit-conversion queries.
* Home-screen and app-drawer folders.
* Android home-screen widgets — add, resize, move, and reconfigure widgets.
* Built-in clock, weather, battery, notification, and People widgets.
* Optional People widget contact matching, with Contacts permission requested only when needed.
* Notification dots and badges, subject to notification access.
* Configurable home-screen gestures, page management, and desktop locking.
* Customizable appearance, including themes, fonts, labels, icon sizes, colors, drawer backgrounds, and third-party icon packs.
* Backup and restore for launcher layout and settings.
* Double-tap empty home-screen space to lock the screen, when enabled.

Privacy and network use: Nomina has no accounts, ads, analytics, or telemetry. Weather is the only feature that uses the internet. When weather is enabled and location permission is granted, the app retrieves weather data from Open-Meteo using rounded coordinates. Place names are resolved locally with Android's geocoder; Nomina does not use an external reverse-geocoding service.
